01 / The method

Three frames in.
Fifty femtoseconds ahead.

At a 50 fs stride in a warm liquid, the next state is a probability distribution rather than a single deterministic point. WignerFlow pairs exact 3D equivariance with conditional flow matching over coasting residuals.

01

Spatial SO(2) + Wigner-D encoder

Each neighbor edge rotates features into a local edge frame via Wigner-D matrices (l ≤ 2). Putting log(envelope) inside softmax with a zero-logit sink makes cutoff crossings smooth.

02

Invariant causal temporal attention

Scalar (l = 0) keys and queries attend across the last k = 3 frames while transporting higher-degree equivariant vectors and quadrupoles without breaking rotation or mirror parity.

03

Residual conditional flow matching

Instead of predicting raw velocity v_new—which systematically damps motion toward 0 K—the flow head integrates corrections relative to constant-velocity coasting (Δx − vΔt, v_new − v).

Post-training stability alignment

Gradual unroll curriculum (m: 2 → 3 → 4), all-step unroll supervision, RepulsionPrior, and SpikeGuard prevent multi-step rollout blowups.

02 / Long-horizon evidence

Does an integrator-free model stay physical?

Every model checkpoint is tested on 15 model-only rollouts of 10,000 steps (0.5 ns each) from held-out test trajectories—with no OpenMM forces, no thermostat, and every training seed reported.

Liquid structure & cage rattling

Accurate RDF and VACF at 50 fs stride.

On the released v0 checkpoint (step 10,000), the Ar–Ar first solvation peak matches OpenMM within 0.025 Å (height error 0.1%), and the velocity autocorrelation zero crossing lands at 305.5 fs vs 307.4 fs truth (0.6% error).
