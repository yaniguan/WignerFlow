03 / Core Architecture

        
Three frames in. Fifty femtoseconds ahead.

        

          At a 50 fs stride in a warm liquid, the next state is a probability distribution rather than a single deterministic point. WignerFlow pairs exact O(3) equivariance with flow matching over constant-velocity coasting residuals.
        

      


      

        

          

            01
            

              
SO(2) Wigner-D edge encoder + smooth cutoff sink

              
Each neighbor edge rotates features (l ≤ 2) into its local edge frame via Wigner-D matrices. Placing log(envelope) inside softmax with a zero-logit sink keeps cutoff crossings C² continuous.

            

          

          

            02
            

              
Invariant causal temporal attention

              
Scalar (l = 0) queries and keys attend across the last k = 3 frames while transporting l = 1 vectors and l = 2 quadrupoles without breaking 3D rotation or mirror parity.

            

          

          

            03
            

              
Residual conditional flow matching

              
Direct velocity prediction damps liquid motion toward 0 K. Predicting corrections relative to coasting (Δx − vΔt, v_new − v) preserves thermal kinetic energy over 10⁴ steps.

            

          

          

            Post-training stability alignment
            
Gradual unroll curriculum (m: 2 → 3 → 4), all-step unroll loss, RepulsionPrior, and SpikeGuard prevent multi-step rollout blowups.

          

        


        
      


      
    


    
    

      

        

          
04 / Measured Long-Horizon Benchmarks

          
Does an integrator-free model stay physical?

          

            Every checkpoint is evaluated on 15 model-only rollouts of 10,000 steps (0.5 ns each) from held-out test trajectories—with no OpenMM forces, no thermostat, and all five seeds reported.
          

        


        

          

            Liquid structure & cage rattling
            
Accurate RDF and VACF at 50 fs stride

            
On the released v0 checkpoint (step 10,000), the Ar–Ar first solvation peak matches OpenMM within 0.025 Å (0.1% height error), and the velocity autocorrelation zero crossing lands at 305.5 fs vs 307.4 fs truth (0.6% error).

          

          

            Multi-seed honesty
            
4 / 5 seeds survive 10⁴ steps; 2 / 5 pass all v0 gates

            
Seeds 3 and 4 hold equilibrium temperatures of 92.2 K and 92.6 K (−2.9% and −2.4% vs 94.9 K ground truth). Seed 2 crashes near step 4,000, motivating stability-first checkpoint selection (best_stable.pt).
