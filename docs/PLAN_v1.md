Written 2026-10-19 on branch `v1.0-dev`. v0.1.0 is released (argon proof of concept). This plan turns it into a paper.

## The paper's claim

Working title: *What makes autoregressive equivariant MD generators stable for 10^4 steps?*

An honest framing, based on the v0 data. Stable rollouts are possible but fragile, and a small set of choices decides it:

1. Residual targets (Δx − vΔt, v_new − v) instead of direct targets.
2. A flow-matching head instead of regression.
3. An unroll horizon capped at ~200 fs.
4. Checkpoint selection with long validation rollouts that include a temperature term, not loss or short RDF-only rollouts.

Each is backed by multi-seed numbers, with 95% intervals over seeds (`scripts/summarize_seeds.py`).

Not claimed: speed-up over classical MD (OpenMM is far faster on argon), energy conservation, or that equivariance helps, until the baseline below shows it.

## Work packages, in order

Each package ends with numbers in `RESULTS.md` and a commit on a branch. Nothing is merged to `main` without a passing test suite.

- **P1 Selection rule.** Done in code and merged into `v1.0-dev` (temperature-aware score, `select_checkpoint.py`, `summarize_seeds.py`). Test evaluation of the new selection for seeds 1 and 2 is running; the result decides whether the rule goes into `main`.
- **P2 Seeds.** Train 5 more flow + residual seeds (6 to 10). Re-select with `select_checkpoint.py`. Report stable fraction and criteria met, with intervals.
- **P3 Ablation arms with 3 seeds each.** Regression vs flow, direct vs residual, horizon 200 vs 400 fs, noise σ, k, lmax, velocity input. One-factor-at-a-time around the default.
- **P4 Non-equivariant baseline.** `equitraj/baselines.py`: a transformer on raw coordinates with the same inputs, residual targets and flow head, matched parameter count. Rotation test shows it is not equivariant. Same 3-seed protocol.
- **P5 Molecules.** Configs and evaluation for alanine dipeptide and aspirin (vacuum, no PBC, bonded crash test, φ/ψ FES, bond statistics). Same protocol, 3 seeds.
- **P6 MACE + Verlet baseline.** Train MACE on the saved forces, integrate with velocity Verlet at the same stride-equivalent cost; compare accuracy per unit wall time. This is the comparison reviewers will ask for.
- **P7 Stride sweep.** n = 1, 5, 10, 50 on argon and ala2.
- **P8 Writing.** LaTeX paper, figures regenerated by script, limits section.

## Honest risks

- The v0 stability is fragile (4 of 5 seeds stable, 2 of 5 meet every criterion). If extra seeds and the new selection rule do not raise this, the paper becomes a stability analysis, which is a smaller but still publishable claim.
- Argon is a weak system for an ML venue. Alanine dipeptide and aspirin are the minimum to show the recipe is not argon-specific.
- A non-equivariant baseline may do as well on argon (isotropic liquid). If so, the equivariance claim moves to molecules, or is dropped.
- Classical-MD systems are a proof of concept; reviewers will ask about the practical payoff. The answer is ab initio MD, which is out of scope for v1.0 and must be stated as such.
- Clusters have bad nodes (g12, m3, g401 and K40-class hosts have failed jobs); job scripts exclude them.

## Rules

KISS, plain PyTorch, English docs, one YAML plus one command per experiment. All numbers in the paper come from `results/` files generated by scripts in the repo.
